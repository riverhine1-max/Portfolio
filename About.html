I’m a student at Meridian Technical Charter High School, interested in game development, software, and building useful ideas for the web.

I started with browser projects and expanded into Unity and C# as my ideas became more ambitious. Gilded Fate brings together my interests in game systems, interface design, and iteration.

I use AI to help brainstorm, write code, and debug. I choose the direction, test the results, and integrate the work. I’m also exploring how my web skills can become a business.

Outside of development, I play varsity tennis for Centennial High School. Practice, patience, and small improvements matter to me in both.
