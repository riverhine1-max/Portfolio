A research direction for making creative development workflows more consistent, inspectable, and easier to iterate on.

Explore the research areas ↘Visit the Lab

This is a research and prototyping project. It is not a released AI product, and no finished model or service is being claimed here.

Research questions

Make each creative
iteration easier to trust.

Asset consistency

Explore ways to evaluate whether a character, prop, or environment remains recognizably consistent across generated concept material.

Prompt and reference evaluation

Compare prompt and reference strategies so a creator can see what improved, what drifted, and what should be preserved.

Provenance and reproducibility

Record creative inputs, decisions, versions, and outputs so a concept can be revisited instead of becoming a one-off result.

Quality review

Investigate practical checks for continuity, accessible presentation, and readiness before work is shown in a public portfolio.

GDD-aware assistance

Research an assistant that can reason from a project’s own design document and flag where new ideas do not yet fit the established direction.

Human direction

Keep creative decisions, testing, and final judgment with the developer. The research supports a workflow; it does not replace authorship.

Initial roadmap

  1. DiscoverChoose a narrow development problem with a measurable outcome.
  2. PrototypeBuild a small, reviewable experiment around that problem.
  3. EvaluateTest the result against consistency, provenance, and practical usefulness.
  4. SharePublish only work that can be explained clearly and honestly.
