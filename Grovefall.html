A samurai action-game concept about a small squirrel warrior crossing a ruined, machine-haunted world toward an awakening Iron Spire.

Design pillars

Precision, momentum,
and a place to explore.

Movement as discovery

Rooftop routes, wall jumps, air dashes, gliding, and slides make paths through the world part of the challenge.

Katana to rifle loop

Connected katana strikes charge the rifle. Spending that charge creates a deliberate choice between close-range pressure and a powerful ranged opening.

Readable danger

Combat centers on dodging, timing, enemy anticipation, and clear consequences. The concept avoids relying on parries or graphic violence.

The world

Ruined villages, shrines, and industrial roots

Ancient timber architecture and overgrown stone sit against corrupted machinery. Lantern villages, forests, ravines, and mountain routes point toward the same impossible landmark.

The Iron Spire

A pagoda-inspired mechanical tower that dwarfs the surrounding mountains. It is the world’s visual anchor and the central unanswered threat.
