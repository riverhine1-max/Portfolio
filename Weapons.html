Game Modes

Your squad size changes everything. Going solo demands precision and caution, while larger teams allow for coordination and aggressive playstyles. Each mode offers a different level of risk, reward, and strategy when facing ARC forces.
Mode Experience Difficulty
Singles Play alone, rely entirely on your own skill and decisions. Alot more calm and less PVP and more PVE. Low
Duos Team up with one partner for balanced combat and strategy. Sometime PVP and sometimes PVE. Moderate
Trios Full squad gameplay with strong coordination and survivability. PVP on first site of other players. High

Tips

Singles: Stay stealthy and avoid unnecessary fights.

Duos: Stick together and cover each other's weaknesses.

Trios: Communicate and assign roles for maximum efficiency.
