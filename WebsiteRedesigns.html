01 The brief

Make the next step clear.

I research each business and rebuild its presentation around clearer calls to action and responsive layouts. The collection includes concepts for Patriot Cleaning, Blast Exterior Cleaning, Boise Home Painting, PAINTCO, and more.

These are independent concepts for outreach, not claims of commissioned client work or business results.

01

Research

Study the business, its services, and what a visitor needs to find first.

02

Restructure

Rebuild the hierarchy around one obvious next step: call, quote, or book.

03

Ship it live

Responsive HTML, CSS, and JavaScript, hosted on GitHub Pages so it can be explored in any browser.

02 The build

From practice to application.

Built with HTML, CSS, and JavaScript, the demos put information hierarchy and responsive design into a real-world context. GitHub Pages hosts the collection so each concept can be explored in a browser.

The goal is to turn web-development practice into business experience through research, implementation, and outreach.
