Arc Raiders Overview

What is Arc Raiders

Arc Raiders is a third-person multiplayer extraction shooter set in a futuristic world where Earth has been invaded by massive machines called ARC. Players take on the role of Raiders, survivors who explore dangerous surface areas to scavenge supplies, complete missions, and fight both robotic enemies and other players. The goal is to survive, collect valuable loot, and successfully escape while working alone or with a team in a hostile, sci-fi environment.

Main Menu

The Main Menu is the first screen players see when launching the game. It serves as the central hub for navigation and game access. From here, players can access:

  1. Main Hub - The main hub is the underground safe area where players prepare for missions and interact with different systems. It acts as the central place to manage gear, access traders, and start raids.
  2. Traders - Traders are NPC vendors who sell weapons, gear, and resources in exchange for currency or items. They may also offer tasks that reward valuable equipment and unlock new items.
  3. Workshop - The workshop lets players craft, upgrade, and modify weapons and equipment. It is essential for improving your loadout and increasing survival chances in raids.
  4. Character - Your character is the Raider you control, including their gear, appearance, and progression. You can customize and strengthen your character to perform better in combat and exploration.
  5. Battle Pass - The battle pass is a progression system where players earn rewards by completing challenges and gaining experience. It includes cosmetics, gear, and other unlockable content over multiple levels.
Visually, the main menu is designed to reflect the theme and aesthetic of the game. For ARC Raiders, this means a futuristic, high-tech interface with sleek buttons, animated backgrounds, glowing effects, and elements that hint at the game's robotic enemies and sci-fi world. It's both functional and immersive, setting the tone for the gameplay experience.
